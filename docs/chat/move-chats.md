# Bring Chats from Another Folder

Moved, renamed or cloned a repository? Bring its chats, plans and Bonfire history to the new folder.

Ember keeps chats per folder. If you move or rename a repository's folder, or clone it somewhere else, its chats stay
filed under the old folder. Bring them over and they appear in the new folder's chat list, ready to continue.

## When Ember offers it

When you open a copy of a repository whose chats are filed under a folder that's gone (or on another drive), the chat
panel asks **Chats for this repository are filed under ‹folder›. Bring them here?** Click **Bring them**, or **Not
now**.

## Bring chats yourself

1. Run **Bring Chats from Another Folder…**, or in **Settings › Chats** click **Bring
   chats from another folder…**.
2. Ember lists the folders that have Ember chats, with how many and when they were last used. Folders of the same
   repository are marked **This repository** and already picked.
3. Pick the folders to bring chats from and press Enter.
4. Confirm with **Bring Them**.

## What moves

- Your chats, with their plans, review state, memories and Bonfire history, move to this folder. The old folder's
  window lists them under **Other folders**.
- Claude Code's transcripts are copied, never moved or overwritten, so Claude can resume the chats here.
- Nothing in either repository folder is changed.

> **Tip:** Chats only show in the folder they're filed under. Chat search (see [Conversation Search](../knowledge/conversation-search.md)) covers
> every folder, so you can find an old chat before you bring it over.

## Moving one chat to another tab

A chat whose branch lives in another worktree moves to that worktree's tab when you ask: **Move this chat to tab 2** on its
branch line, **Move to tab 2** in its menu, or **Move This Chat to Its Worktree's Tab** from the Command Palette. Its plan
and its stopped or finished Bonfire run go with it (see [Move a chat to its worktree's tab](../worktrees/worktrees.md#move-a-chat-to-its-worktrees-tab)). A worktree's
tab also offers the chats other tabs run in its folder, with **Move here**. Chats on the same branch as their tab don't
move. To work on a chat of another open tab, click **Open in tab 2** to bring that tab to the front (see
[A chat that belongs to another tab](../worktrees/across-tabs.md#a-chat-that-belongs-to-another-tab)). To have a chat's branch in front of you, open the branch in its
tab from the branches board or the Dashboard (see [Open a branch in its tab](../worktrees/workspace-tabs.md#open-a-branch-in-its-tab)).
